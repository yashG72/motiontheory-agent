## About us
- **MotionTheory** is a storytelling and video production studio for brands.
- Tagline: **"Visuals | Ideas | Impact."** Brand stories, told to millions.
- We help brands take their story to the masses. We handle everything from the first idea to the final cut: the story, the script, the production and the edit.
- Most brands juggle a writer, a production house and an editor. We are all three, and we plan every film around who it needs to reach and where it will run.
- We are a young, remote team of storytellers, scriptwriters, filmmakers, editors and visual artists who make ads people actually want to watch.
- Our three pillars:
  - **Visuals:** every frame is composed. Light, colour, type and pace are chosen on purpose.
  - **Ideas:** we write the story before we make a single image. If the idea doesn't hold up on paper, it won't hold up on screen.
  - **Impact:** an ad exists to move people. We measure our work by what it does for the brand.

## Services
All of our videos are made for brands (ads, brand films, Reels, Shorts and social content).
- **Motion graphics videos:** animated brand videos, explainers, product and promo animations.
- **AI cinematic visuals videos:** cinematic ad films made with AI-driven visuals, with consistent characters, products and lighting, and every shot directed.
- **Video editing:** cut, colour grade, sound and captions, shaped for every screen (Reels, Shorts, YouTube, ads).
- **Scripting:** the opening hook, the voiceover and a scene-by-scene shot list.
- **Storytelling:** finding the story only your brand can tell, and the one feeling the ad should leave behind.
- **Full campaigns:** all of the above, from first idea to final cut.

## Pricing
- **Motion graphics video:** USD 20–30 per video.
- **AI cinematic visuals video:** USD 50–60 per video.
- The exact price depends on the video's length, complexity and how many videos are needed. The team confirms the final quote after understanding the brief.
- Video editing, scripting, storytelling and full campaigns: custom quote. Hand these to the team for pricing.
- Never offer discounts or bundle prices on your own. Hand those requests to the team.

## How we work
1. **Story:** we dig into the brand, its audience and the one feeling the ad should leave behind.
2. **Script:** every second is written: the hook, the voiceover and a scene-by-scene shot list.
3. **Make:** we produce it the way the story needs: with AI-driven visuals, motion graphics, on set with a crew, or a mix. Every shot is directed.
4. **Edit:** cut, colour, sound and captions, then shaped for every screen the audience watches.

To start, a client only needs a rough idea. Useful things to learn from them: their brand and what it sells, which service they want, how many videos, video length, where it will run (Instagram, YouTube, ads), deadline, and budget.

## What a good lead looks like
- A brand or business (not an individual looking for a job) that wants one or more videos made.
- Knows roughly which service they need, and has a budget that fits our prices above.
- Has a timeline, or a campaign or launch coming up.
- Brands that need several videos or ongoing monthly content are especially valuable.
